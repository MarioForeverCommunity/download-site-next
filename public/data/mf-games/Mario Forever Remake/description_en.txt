01. Info
"Mario Forever Remake", as you are going to play, is the remake version of "Mario Forever" (first released in 2003 (Michael Gdaniec's item)).
The major purpose of this project is to restore the characteristics of the original game, enhance the fluency of the game, and also improve the quality of it through reducing defects and fixing bugs.
The remake version of Mario Forever also contains five new worlds, named World 9, World 10, World 12, World 13 and World U, which are not available in the original game.

*Insturctions for the additional levels mentioned above:
World 9, World 10 are made by syzxchulun (A team member of Mario Forever Remake Group, as the director of Mario Forever Remake project).
World 12, World 13 are made by Mario Forever Remake Group (China).
World U is made by Nmnmoooh.

World 9 is a Mario Forever project made by Syzxchulun in 2013 ("World 9" in Mario Forever Remake is the simplified version of the original levels first released in 2012).
World 10 is a Mario Forever project made by Syzxchulun in 2013.
World 12 is greatly modified from the previous version of World 12 first released in 2015. Version 3.5 mainly reduces the difficulty and enhance its fun.  
World 13 is newly made for the release of this game.
World U is actually the Mario Forever Project "World U (Easy mode)" made by Nmnmoooh.

03. How to Play this Game 

*The recommended keys are:
Z: Jump / Start a level
X: Run / Shoot Fireballs
Left / Right: Move / Run (with "X" pressed) / Enter a horizontal pipe
Up / Down: Crouch / Change a option / Enter a vertical pipe
Enter: Select an option

*You can change all the settings in "options" as your preferences.
