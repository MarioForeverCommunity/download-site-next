Mario Forever Compass Rose is the result of the inspiration I've received this year thanks to a very special person. I've learned that life should be lived without worrying about nonexistent anxieties, which are only in our heads. We should be less paranoid and more instinctive in the difficult choices life presents us. The outcome of our choices won't be a failure if we don't give up. Mario isn't afraid either, and even though he doesn't know which path to take to save his beloved, he follows his instincts and feels he's getting closer and closer to his goal.
