This game is a kind of homage to Mario Forever: Advance Editon. Here you'll play 5 worlds that will drive you crazy because of their
insane difficulty. If you beat them, it means that you're a MF pro player!

===============================================================
03. Controls

X = Run/Shoot fireballs
Z = Jump/Start level
Left and right arrows = Move left/right
CTRL+J = Hide top bar
CTRL+H = Show top bar
