[Game introduction]

-Main levels-

MRI2 has 4 chapter,every chapter has some levels(the number of every chapter is different).
Also, there is a "!" level in every chapter.It means more difficult or more exploration.
In save game room,you can see the "Hidden Backrooms" 2-B and 3-B,but they are locked.The two levels are all in a very small backroom,and you can only use the object in the backroom to escape there.The entrance of 2-B is in 2-1 and the entrance of 3-B is in 3-6.
So,let me list the number of levels in every chapter:
Chapter 1 has 6+1 levels.
Chapter 2 has 8+1+1 levels.
Chapter 3 has 14+1+1 levels.
Chapter 4 has 10+1 levels.

Besides,you can choose the chapter when you are in Main Map.

-Other levels-

1.Past World
It is made of five typical levels in my old product MRI.Some of them are revised.The theme of Past World is grassland and forest.

2.Bowser Arena
It is the palce to fight with Bowser.Now only "Bowser Tower" is accessible.There are 25 floors.The toppest floor you beat will be recorded.

3.Mini Game
There are 4 Mini Game in save game room.The number of unlocked Mini Game depends on how many chapters you unlocked.By playing the Mini Game,you can get a lot of coins.You can use the coins to buy something in coins store.



[Design and innovation]

1.Special Maps
The maps of MRI2 are unusual.There are some branches in the map and you can choose the way and the level you want to play.The way you unlocked will be saved,it means every level can be keep on a file.It makes the whole game easier.

2.Coins Store
In MRI2,when you collect 100 coins,it won't add one life to Mario.You should use the coins in Coins Store.Not only Mario's life but also some supplies can be bought there.

——Main Map——

press any key:go to save game room
