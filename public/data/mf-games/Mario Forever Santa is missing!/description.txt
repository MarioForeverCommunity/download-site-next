		The Forever Team presents: Mario Forever: Santa is missing!

===============================================================
01. Infos

This is a Mario Forever game entirely Christmas themed; someone stolen Santa Claus... or who might be him. Mario will encounter some levels of the land where the mysterious thief walked through, or better, swimmed through: the Christmas Lake. All levels are snowy and water themed. Enjoy this little cool adventure and rescue the lost Christmas to the Mushroom Kingdom!

===============================================================
02. Controls

X = Run/Shoot fireballs
Z = Jump/Start level
Left and right arrows = Move left/right
CTRL+J = Hide top bar
CTRL+H = Show top bar
