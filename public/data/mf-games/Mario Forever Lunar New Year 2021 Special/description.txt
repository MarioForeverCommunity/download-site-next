THANK YOU FOR DOWNLOADING MARIO FOREVER: LUNAR NEW YEAR 2021 SPECIAL VERSION 2.0!

"Mario Forever: Lunar New Year 2021 Special" is the 2021 sequel of MF:LNY 2020. This project is a fanmade version of the original "Mario Forever", a Super Mario fanmade game created by a Polish creator, legend Michal "Buziol" Gdaniec made with Multimedia Fusion 1.2.
This project is made with Clickteam Fusion 2.5+.

1. Version general info
"MF:LNY 2021 v2.0" contain 5 main worlds and extra levels by JohnzVN & JoséLPK.CF&MF.
