 Notes: - The level is an ~2:30 minutes long autoscrolling level with tons of fiery hazzards. The difficulty is meant to be similar
	with endgame (World 8+) difficulty, so it may require a few attempts until you reach the goal. Despite it being a stronghold/
	fortress level, it doesn't have a boss (yet). The level was inspired from Toad Strikes Back's last castle level, "The Koopa
	Curse", an autoscrolling level. Have fun! :)
