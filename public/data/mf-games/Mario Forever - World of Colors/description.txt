1. INFORMATION
World of Colors is served as an extra world in Mario Forever: Inversive Worlds. The arrangement of each level based on
color arrangement of a rainbow. There are 7 levels - 7 colors to play with, featuring:
World of Colors-1: Lavatic Lair
World of Colors-2: Sunset Delight
World of Colors-3: Pyramid Ruins
World of Colors-4: Forest Wreck
World of Colors-5: Jumpy Sky Fly
World of Colors-6: Sleeting Night Showdown
World of Colors-7: Poisonous Castle

4. BASIC KEYS
X: Run/Shoot fireballs
Z: Jump/Start a level
ENTER: Start the game
Left/Right keys: Move left/right.
Up/Down keys: Enter vertical pipes
F2: Restart the game
F3: Go to Save Game Room
F4: Go to Main Menu
