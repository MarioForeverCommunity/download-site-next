01. STANDARD vs. DIRECT 3D 9

The game comes with two versions: Standard display and Direct 3D 9 display.

Standard display mode is the classic method which displays all common stuff like original MF games, but this might income in lags for some specific PCs.
This mode is recommended generally for any PC, aside some cases where most recent PCs doesn't support old screen mode.

Direct 3D 9 display mode shows less effects and candy-eye graphics in the game, but it might run better without incoming lags in the aforementioned specific PCs.
This mode is recommended specifically for most recent PCs.

If you're unsure which mode to choose, use the Standard mode.

03. CONTROLS

X = Run/Shoot fireballs
Z = Jump/Start level
Left and right arrows = Move left/right
Left and right arrows = Choose Mario or Luigi (Main Menu only)
CTRL+J = Hide top bar
CTRL+H = Show top bar
1 (Keyboard) / 4 (Num. Keyboard) = Choose a letter world
2 (Keyboard) / 6 (Num. Keyboard) = Choose a letter world
