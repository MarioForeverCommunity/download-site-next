I played many MF fangames, and I thought, 'What if I made my own fangame?'
It started, plainly on my curiousness, and I have been making this for some months ago.

Just because something doesn't do what you planned it to do doesn't mean it's useless.
\- Thomas Edison

I continued developing, no matter how hard it was; having no time, thinking to make creative levels...

* * *

My worlds and levels have one idea: "Don't waste time!"

**MARIO FOREVER  -  TIME CHALLENGE WORLDS**

**_There is no greater gift than time. - Jonathan Safran Foer_**

These "Time Challenge Worlds" have:
\- Short but demanding levels, but with lesser cheapness
\- Three different themed worlds that contain new challenges
\- Levels with diversive powerups (Using all: Flower, Beetroot, Jumping Lui, Gear)

Contents

\-Abnormal Save Game Room
\-Lots of extra levels; some are levels I made individually, some contain new unique challenges
\-Three worlds with colorful themes
\-Survival Mode for hardcore players
