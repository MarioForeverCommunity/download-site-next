
  =-= TheMarioVariable presents: Mario Forever Collection =-=

This little application is a bundle of all Mario Forever games I've developed in ten years and counting. You can either decide to open the executable I made for a candy-eye play, or simply go to "games" folder and choose the game you want to play by name.

Mario Forever projects include:

*Mario Worker: Oldest World
*Mario Worker: Letter Worlds Series (Worlds I to X)
*Mario Forever Speedrun
*Mario Forever Remake
*Mario Worker Remake
*Mario Forever All-Stars
*Mario Forever Fanmade
*Mario Forever Revolution
*Mario Forever: Hardcore Distopia
