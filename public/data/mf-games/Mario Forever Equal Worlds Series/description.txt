--- Mario Forever: Equal Worlds Series ---
Developed by: Xanderstar1456
Engine by: Lyh and Szxychulun

This idea was originally planned in 2020 and some levels are made with Mario Worker Remake v3.03 by TheMarioVariable. After discovering an engine made by Lyh and Szxychulun, I was planning to remake those levels and put it in that engine. But unfortunately, some objects in the engine are not complete and they have to be made in order to finish those levels. So, with my struggle to modify the engine, I have completed it and here's the journey that started. :)
