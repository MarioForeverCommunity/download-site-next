Please make sure the windows of your PC is at least windows 10. Otherwise, you will encounter an error.

If your PC does not support Vulkan or Direct3D 12, please consider running via "Run in Compatibility Mode.bat".

If your PC does not support Direct3D 12 but supports Vulkan, please run the game via "Run in Vulkan.bat"

Due to the fact that running from bat will allow you to check the console of the game, you can help with feedbacks based on the information in the console.
