Hi-Fire, dear gamer!! Thank you for wanting to play my game.

This game oriented to beginner and amateur gamers and have "Save progress" after reaching every of levels (except Final Boss Battle, here game after its ending will starts from stage "Night-5").

If you want to activate Sounds and Music - choose "Options" in main menu and activate them.

If you want to skip the main and final cutscenes - press "Enter".

If you want to pause the game - press "P" and "Enter" to continue your gaming.

So, I wish you a pleasant gaming, and, I hope, you will pass this game without any problem. With respect to you, Firecat!! ;)
