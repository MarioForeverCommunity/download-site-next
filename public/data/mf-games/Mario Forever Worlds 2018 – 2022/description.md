Hello everyone, today I am releasing my new game that brings together all my works made in the years 2018 – 2022. It is available for Android and Windows platforms. It contains my Greek worlds from the Adventure in the Greek worlds series and in addition to some extra and custom worlds that I made as my world 13 is its 2016 version.

I also added 14 extra levels that some were shown in other games. The game has a medium to high difficulty and it is possible that you will complete it in a few days.

**Comment of the levels and worlds:**

-   World Alpha: I had in mind that this world should be my world 15, but seeing its potential I thought of doing something bigger.
-   World Beta: The first level of this world I had in mind that the Banzai Bill would appear but I had not made that graphic yet so I forgot, at the end of 2022 I have made a change in the levels, I did not release this version because I was busy.
-   World Gamma: This is my favorite world for its graphics and music, it has a hard difficulty, so you have to be careful with the Beetroot Bro and Jumping Koopa Orange.
-   World Delta: I like this world because of its difficulty, I advise you to have several lives before reaching the pyramid. I made some light changes in this new version.
-   World 13 Classic 2016: I thought I had lost this world forever but I was surprised that I found a draft of my world 13 in its first version on my aunt’s computer. There were only 2 complete levels, so I had to recreate the other 2 levels from memory from something I did 6 years ago.
-   World 2 Desert version: This version of this world was presented by the year 2018, I can say that it is difficult.
-   World 1 from MWA: I present it together with the version of Mario Worker Android 1.0, I made a few changes to it.
-   World 6 Ice version: I created this version in the year 2021 I think, I’m not sure, it’s my favorite world in that version because you can turn into an ice cube.
-   Lost Way: This version was featured in World 11 Edition 2.0.
-   Out of the Dark: This level makes me travel in time. It is a difficult and fun level,
-   Rain of Rocks: I finished this level in 2019 and I did not present it because I did not know in which world to add it and I forgot its existence.
-   Snowed: Mainly this level was not snowy, but over the years I changed it. I was going to introduce it in the Ice version of Mario Forever but I didn’t because the level didn’t have ice.
-   Cannons: I had the idea that this level was going to be part of a world 15.
-   Spines: It was also going to be part of my world 15 but I forgot.
-   World 2 – 5 Revenge version: It was featured in the World 11 Edition. It is a difficult version especially for the final part, you have to be strategic.
-   Red Sky: It was made for Mario Worker Android 0.98.0.
-   Guns: It was made for Mario Worker Android 0.98.0.
-   Flying Cheep Cheep: It was made for Mario Worker Android 0.98.0 or 1.0?.
-   Maze: It was made for Mario Worker Android 1.0. It was made to test special screen movements.
-   Lost Castle: It was made for Mario Worker Android 1.0.
-   Toxic Level (2022 version): This version has a few changes compared to the version I released in 2018.
-   Night in the Desert: This level was going to be part of the Delta World but it was incomplete.
