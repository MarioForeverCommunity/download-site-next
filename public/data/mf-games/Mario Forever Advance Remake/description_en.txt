01. Info
"Mario Forever Advance Edition Remake", as you are going to play, is the remake version of "Mario Forever Advance" (first released in 2009 (Michael Gdaniec's item)), a harder version of the well-known game "Mario Forever".
The major purpose of this project is to restore the characteristics of the original game, enhance the fluency of the game, and also improve the quality of it through reducing defects and fixing bugs.
The remake version of Mario Forever Advance also contains five new worlds, named World 9 Advanced Edition, World 10 Advanced Edition, World 12 Advanced Edition, World 13 Advanced Edition and World U Advanced Edition, which are not available in the original game.

*Insturctions for the additional levels mentioned above:
World 9, World 10 and their Advanced Edition are made by syzxchulun (A team member of Mario Forever Remake Group, as the director of Mario Forever Remake project).
World 12, World 13 and their Advanced Edition are made by Mario Forever Remake Group (China).
World U and its Advanced Edition is made by Nmnmoooh.


World 9 Advanced edition is the primary Mario Forever project made by Syzxchulun ("World 9" in Mario Forever Remake is the simplified version of the previous work).
World 10 Advanced edition is a Mario Forever project made by Syzxchulun in 2016.
World 12 Advanced edition is newly made for the release of this game.
World 13 Advanced edition is newly made for the release of this game.
World U Advanced edition is actually the Mario Forever Project "World U (Normal mode)" made by Nmnmoooh.

03. How to Play this Game 

*The recommended keys are:
Z: Jump / Start a level
X: Run / Shoot Fireballs
Left / Right: Move / Run (with "X" pressed) / Enter a horizontal pipe
Up / Down: Crouch / Change a option / Enter a vertical pipe
Enter: Select an option

*You can change all the settings in "options" as your preferences.
