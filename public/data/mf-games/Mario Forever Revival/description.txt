02. Project Overview

Hello everyone, I am happy to share with you my newest game: Mario Forever Revival.
Unlike my previous productions, this time around I aimed to create something, that
will be accessible in terms of difficulty level to broader spectrum of MF players.
Furthermore, with this game I tried to stay closer to classic Mario Forever by Buziol,
while introducing some new enemies and gimmicks that fit general style. I hope you will
have fun time playing this one.

This game was also developed under updated version of the engine in which I made my previous games:
Mario Forever: Powerup Engine. Here are some of the key features that were edited in comparison to
what you could experience in my other productions:
-springboard physics are more lenient: you can now jump off them like any regular enemy,
-you can bounce of Bowser higher than before - jumping height depends on whether you hold jump buton,
-additional collisions were coded: for example beetroots can now hit [?] blocks, koopa shells can defeat piranha plants and so on,
-enemy collision detections was slightly improved - you should experience less often now situations when enemy goes through solid blocks.
