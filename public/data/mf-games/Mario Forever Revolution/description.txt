01. Infos
Development for Mario Forever Revolution started in late 2015. I wanted to make something completely different from the usual
stuff always seen in classic MF games. I've started to check and test around some codes and then I've started to build and create
new things like new characters, new objects like the P-Switches and the Note Blocks, and even new cocnepts for levels and maps,
while keeping the original and classic visual style, with some redefinitions of course.

Almost one year later, to celebrate my birthday, I've showed worldwide the very first prototype of the game, showing Peach and Toad
as fully playable characters, new objects, new powerups, new mechanics and more. Neverless the long wait, I'm is still in pre-alpha
stages, with a lot of stuff incomplete, but I'm continuing to work on it for fixing, changing and adding stuff to it.
My main goal is to make MFRevolution a real "revolution"!

===============================================================

02. Controls

X = Run/Shoot fireballs
Z = Jump/Start level
Left and right arrows = Move left/right
Up and down arrows = Enter in pipes
Space bar = Panoramic of the map (map only)
CTRL+J = Hide top bar
CTRL+H = Show top bar
CTRL+P/Enter = Pause
