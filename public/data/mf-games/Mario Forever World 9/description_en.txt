Hi, you are about to play Mario Forever World 9 made by fans, syzxchulun.
   I always love Mario Games, and I always want to create some custom levels just like Mario Forever.
   So now you can see I made it on Multimedia Fusion 1.5.
   In this additional world, you will see some custom graphics which I picked from Mario Forever 2.16, maybe it's a kind
   of old but I'm sure they will bring you familiar and special memories of Mario Forever games.

In this world, there are 4 different kinds of levels,and some of elements you can't find them in the custom MF.
For example, I create a volcano theme in the third level of this world, and plenty of new enemies and special blocks as well.

This game is called "World 9", so maybe the levels are not easy to beat. If you feel too hard to finish my world,
complete 8 basic worlds in Mario Forever for the first:)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to control:
Press any key to start!
Button "Z"to jump, "X" to fire/run.
