THANK YOU FOR DOWNLOADING MARIO FOREVER: LUNAR NEW YEAR 2022 - THE ULTIMATE VOYAGE VERSION 3.0!

"Mario Forever: Lunar New Year 2022 - The Ultimate Voyage" is the 2022 and final continuation of the Lunar New Year series. This project is a fanmade version of the original "Mario Forever", a Super Mario fanmade game created by a Polish creator, legend Michal "Buziol" Gdaniec made with Multimedia Fusion 1.2.
This project is made with Multimedia Fusion 2.

1. Version general info
"MF:LNY 2022 v3.0" contain 11 main worlds and 2 extra worlds (Engine Mineral - EM, Snowy Memories, SM).
