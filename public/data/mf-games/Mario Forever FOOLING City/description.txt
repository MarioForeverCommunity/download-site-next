When I was making this video, to present a really poorly translated text, I searched for many translators and finally find one. Nowadays, translators are highly intelligent, but for those "idiot translators" finder, It's not good news lol.
And why "falling city"? This comes from my playing "survival game" with my friends (That's a game that gives you a story and many options, some options may lead to death of the character, and your aim is to survive as many rounds as possible), and I wrote a story where almost every sign can fall and hit the character. That gave me the inspiration of this level.
