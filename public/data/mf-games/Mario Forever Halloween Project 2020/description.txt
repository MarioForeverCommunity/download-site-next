		The Forever Team presents: Mario Forever: Halloween Project 2020

===============================================================
01. Infos

This is a Mario Forever game entirely Halloween themed; you'll encounter spooky levels, scary enemies and deadful boss battles!
Every level has been built by a member of the team, each with its own style and thematic.

Each world has a proper theme:
World 1 - Pumpkin Kingdom
World 2 - Haunted Hills
World 3 - Dark Deadlands

We hope you enjoy the game, and Happy Halloween (even if a bit late :p)

===============================================================
02. Controls

X = Run/Shoot fireballs
Z = Jump/Start level
Left and right arrows = Move left/right
CTRL+J = Hide top bar
CTRL+H = Show top bar
