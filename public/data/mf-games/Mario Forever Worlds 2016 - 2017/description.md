## **Mario Forever Worlds 2016 – 2017**

Here is a project that contains many of my works, such as worlds, levels, re-versions made in the years 2016 – 2017.

### **World 13 | Frustrating**

This was my second job done in the years 2015 to 2016, I was inspired by worlds 9 and 10 to create my world. His first version of this world was made with the Mario Forever Engine project by Dunami’s. For this second version I had to redo 4 other levels because I lost the world project 13. It is very difficult to win this world …

### **World 14 | Very hard**

In 2017 I released my project World 14, a dark world with difficult levels, initially this world was going to be called Dark World, but I decided that this world is the continuation of world 13.

This world is very difficult to play, just win it a few times. It is exciting to get to the last level.

### **World Alternative | My special world**

This world collects all the bonus levels that were released with my projects, it also includes some unpublished.

### **Bonus levels and worlds**

Here I describe a bit about those bonuses.

**1\. Green Sunset**
It was included as an additional level for the first version of my world 13, it is a tropical level. I had to create it again because world project 13 was lost.

**2\. Volcano Lotus**
I really like this level, when I finished creating it I thought about creating a world of volcanoes, something I couldn’t do because I didn’t have time.

**3\. Example Medium
**This level was created in 2016 after playing a lot of minecraft in the dimension of “Nether”, I will only say that it is a good level.

**4\. Night Strange**
This level was inspired by the level “goomba party” and Mario Forever World U from “nmnoooh”.

**5\. Sandstorm**
This level was going to be much darker, and the sand was going to be dirt, but I changed it because I didn’t really like its pale color.
**
6\. Death Valley**
The hardest level you could play, you have to reach that level with many lives.

**7\. World Toxic 1 – 1**
It was created in late December 2017, my inspiration after reading about the Chernobyl accident.

**8\. World 5 (We’re Clones Version)**
Ever thought what would happen if the enemies of all the worlds of Mario forever were clones. Well I did that with world 5 because I had no idea what Bowser would be like.

**9\. World 1 (Snow Version)**
Another experiment of “how could it be”, world 1 was changed its graphics to others with snow.

**10\. Versions of levels**
These are demos of the levels for world 13, these levels are very difficult.
